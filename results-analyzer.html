06 · ELECTROCHEMICAL PARAMETERS

Energy-difference calculator

Enter total energies from separate, consistent neutral/cation/anion calculations. Use the same method and carefully specify vertical or adiabatic definitions.

Uses IP = E(cation) − E(neutral), EA = E(neutral) − E(anion), η = (IP − EA)/2, μ = −(IP + EA)/2 and ω = μ²/(2η), converting Eh to eV. These are finite-difference descriptors under the stated convention.
